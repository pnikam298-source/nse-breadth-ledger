    Fresh highs against fresh lows

    How many stocks traded beyond their highest (right) or lowest (left) price of the last 1 and 6 months and 52 weeks. Highs and lows are intraday, so a stock counts the moment it trades past its old extreme.

    The last 60 sessions

    One square per day, oldest on the left, today on the right. Teal means broad strength, clay means broad weakness. Monthly thrust counts stocks up 25% or more in 20 sessions, minus those down 25% or more.

    Where fresh highs cluster

    Industries ranked by the share of their stocks at a 3-month high today (dark bar). The pale bar is the share at a 1-month high. Only industries with at least three tracked stocks are shown.

    Broad momentum

    Pick an industry, a gain and a number of trading days. The page counts how many of its stocks gained at least that much and lists them, so you can tell a broad advance from a few stocks doing the work. Click a name to open its chart on TradingView.
    Every industry, ranked by the share of its stocks that meet the same test. Click one to open it above.

    52-week highs

    Stocks at a new high today, or sitting close to their 52-week high. Highs are measured on the intraday price. Choose what to list, set how close counts as close (anything from 0.5% to 30%), and narrow it to one industry if you like. Click a name to open its chart on TradingView.

    Session log

    The last 10 sessions, newest first.

    Position sizer

    Work out how many shares to buy from your stop-loss and how much of your account you are willing to risk. It runs in your browser; nothing you type is sent anywhere.
    Quick stop
    Tap a number in a target box to change it.
    A calculator, not a recommendation. The account size and the other figures you type stay in this browser and are never sent anywhere.
