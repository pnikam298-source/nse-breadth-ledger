Quantitative Methodology & Mathematical Definitions

Comprehensive documentation of data sources, backward price adjustment algorithms, survivorship disclosures, and breadth calculations.

1. Primary Data Sources & Immutable Raw Storage

To eliminate data errors caused by web scraping, synthetic estimates, or unofficial aggregators, NSE Breadth Ledger strictly ingests exchange-published files:

  • Official End-of-Day Bhavcopy: National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) Common Bhavcopy in current UDiFF (Unified Data Interchange Financial Format) format (BhavCopy_NSE_CM_0_0_0_YYYYMMDD_F_0000.csv.zip). Includes official Open, High, Low, Close, PrevClose, Total Traded Volume, and Total Turnover.
  • Index Constituents & Industry Classification: Sourced directly from official snapshots on niftyindices.com (e.g. ind_nifty500list.csv).
  • Corporate Actions: NSE corporate actions register recording ex-dates for stock splits, bonus issues, rights offerings, and demergers.
  • Trading Holiday Master: Official NSE trading calendar.

Immutable Storage Policy: Every downloaded raw file is stored permanently under /data/raw/ with its SHA-256 cryptographic checksum recorded in data/metadata/raw_checksums.json. Files are never modified or overwritten in place.

2. Universe Selection & Survivorship Bias Disclosure

The tracked universe represents the active constituents of the chosen index (default: Nifty 500), filtered by the following criteria:

SeriesEQ onlyExcludes debt securities (NX, N1), preference shares, and REITs/InvITs (RR) to reflect pure common equity breadth.
Minimum Price₹10.00Filters out illiquid penny stocks susceptible to pump-and-dump manipulation.

History LengthAt least 252 trading sessionsRequired to construct valid, non-distorted 52-week (252 sessions) high and low metrics.

Survivorship Bias Disclosure

The universe utilizes dated index constituent snapshots from niftyindices.com. In historical backtests, stocks that were removed from the Nifty 500 prior to the current snapshot date are not retrospectively reconstructed. This creates survivorship bias in multi-year historical data. For daily breadth tracking and positional risk assessment, today's advance/decline breadth reflects the current investable universe.

3. Backward Price Adjustment & Quarantine Engine

Unadjusted splits and bonus issues create massive false "52-week lows" that corrupt market breadth. To guarantee correctness:

Daily Adjustment Ratio: R_t = PrevClose_t / Close_{t-1}
  • On normal trading days with no corporate actions, PrevClose_t == Close_{t-1}, yielding R_t = 1.0.
  • When a corporate action (e.g. 1:1 bonus or 2:1 split) occurs, the exchange adjusts PrevClose_t to the reference baseline (e.g., ₹1,000 becomes ₹500, ratio = 0.50).
  • Whenever |R_t - 1.0| > 0.01 (1% gap), the event is cross-referenced with the NSE corporate actions table.
  • If verified, the adjustment factor is propagated backwards to all prior sessions:
    adj_Price_{τ} = Price_{τ} × ∏_{s > τ} R_s
  • Quarantine Protocol: If a stock displays an unexplained price gap exceeding 20% without an official corporate action on record, the stock is immediately QUARANTINED. It is isolated from daily breadth counts and logged in the Data Quality Report until resolved.

4. Quantitative Metric Definitions

A. Breakout Highs and Lows

A stock records a new breakout high today if its current adjusted High meets or exceeds the highest adjusted High across the previous $N$ trading sessions (excluding today):

New N-Session High = (adj_High_t ≥ max_{i=1...N} adj_High_{t-i})
New N-Session Low = (adj_Low_t ≤ min_{i=1...N} adj_Low_{t-i})

B. Gain Over N Sessions

Gain_N = (adj_Close_t / adj_Close_{t-N}) - 1.0

C. Monthly Thrust

Measures market thrust velocity by contrasting strong runners against severe decliners over a 20-session trading month:

Monthly Thrust = Count(Gain_20 ≥ +25%) - Count(Gain_20 ≤ -25%)

D. Industry Cluster Concentration

For each industry containing at least 3 tracked stocks:

Share at 3M High (%) = [Count(Stock at 3M High) / Total Industry Stocks] × 100
Share at 1M High (%) = [Count(Stock at 1M High) / Total Industry Stocks] × 100

E. Market Regime Heatmap Score

The daily market regime value maps multi-timeframe net expansion onto a normalised scale $[-1.0, +1.0]$:

Composite Net = (NH_52W - NL_52W) + 0.50 × (NH_6M - NL_6M) + 0.25 × (NH_1M - NL_1M)
Regime Score = Composite Net / Universe Size

F. Client-Side Position Sizer

Risk Per Share (RPS) = Entry × (Stop_Loss_% / 100)
Quantity_Risk = ⌊ (Account × Risk_%) / RPS ⌋
Quantity_Cap = ⌊ (Account × Max_Capital_Cap_%) / Entry ⌋
Final Quantity = min(Quantity_Risk, Quantity_Cap)

5. Regulatory Compliance & Disclaimers

SEBI Non-Advisory Notice

Under SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014 and SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013, NSE Breadth Ledger operates purely as a quantitative market information dashboard. It does not provide buy, sell, or target recommendations. Breadth measures systemic risk and market participation; it is not investment advice.

DPDP Act 2023 Privacy Policy

In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 of India:

  • Contact details (Name, Email, WhatsApp number) are collected strictly with explicit, opt-in consent for market briefing notifications.
  • Users may withdraw consent or request complete data erasure at any time.
  • Position sizer inputs and calculations run entirely in the client's local browser memory and are never transmitted to any external server.
